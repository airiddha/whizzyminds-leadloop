FAQ

Straight answers.

Is the reply marked as automatic?
Yes, always. The first reply says it is automated, gives your booking link, and includes an opt-out line. A person follows up on anything complex.
What does “under a minute” mean?
It is our design target for the first reply to a web-form or email enquiry once your workflow is live. We test with sample leads and show you the timing before you go live. It is a target, not a guarantee, and it depends on your form and email providers.
Who writes the replies?
We draft them with AI help and tune them to your tone. You approve the exact wording before anything is sent to a real lead. Lead scoring is rule-based and explained to you, not a black box.
Do you need my passwords?
No. You grant limited access through your own accounts, and you can revoke it whenever you like.
Can I cancel or switch it off?
Yes. You can switch the automation off at any time, and the optional care plan is month to month.
Do you have customer reviews yet?
Not yet. LeadLoop is a new service line, which is why the first five clients get founding prices. We will only publish reviews from real clients, with their permission.
