What the reply asks

Tell a ready buyer from a browser

We set these up with you. These are a common starting point.

  • Buying, selling or both?So seller appraisal requests stand out straight away.
  • Which area or property?The listing they asked about, or the suburbs they're looking in.
  • What's the timeline?Ready now, in the next three months, or just looking.
  • Finance sorted?For buyers, whether they're pre-approved. Only if you want to ask it.

An example reply

Friendly, useful, and sounds like you.

It never gives a price opinion or promises anything about a property. That stays with you.

FromRachel at Harbour RealtySubjectRe: 14 Elm Street enquiry

Hi Chris, thanks for your interest in 14 Elm Street. I'm out at an inspection right now, so here's a quick note so you're not left waiting.

To make sure I send you the right details, could you tell me:
1. Are you buying to live in or to invest?
2. Do you have a home to sell first?
3. When are you hoping to move?

Or pick a time for a quick call or a private viewing here: book a time. Talk soon, Rachel

An example only. Harbour Realty is made up.

Why speed matters

A study published in Harvard Business Review in 2011 found that companies who responded to an online enquiry within an hour were about 7 times more likely to have a meaningful conversation with the customer than those who waited even one hour longer.

Source: Oldroyd, McElheran & Elkington, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", HBR, March 2011.

FAQ

Questions real estate agents ask

Does it work with portal enquiries?

If the portal sends enquiries to your email inbox, yes. We connect the inbox, so those get the same instant reply.

Will it give price opinions?

No. It never comments on price or value. It thanks them, asks your questions and offers your booking link.

Can each agent in an office have their own replies?

Yes. Each agent can have their own wording, alerts and booking link. Ask on a call how that's priced.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any month, by replying to any email. No contract.
