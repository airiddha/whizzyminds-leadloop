The problem

Enquiries go cold quickly. A Harvard Business Review study of 1.25 million sales leads found that firms contacting a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to have a meaningful conversation with the decision maker as firms that waited one hour more. In the same article's mystery-shopper test, 23% of companies never responded at all. Read the study summary. The data is from 2011, and results for your business will vary.

What you get

Instant first reply

Every web-form enquiry gets a clear, friendly message that says it is an automated first reply and that a real person will follow up.

Simple lead scoring

Transparent rules (urgency, budget mentioned, phone number, clear request) mark each lead hot, warm or cold. You see the reasons, not a black box.

Booking link

The reply includes your own booking link, such as Calendly or Google Calendar appointment pages.

Owner alert and follow-up

Hot leads alert you straight away. Every lead gets one polite follow-up after 24 hours. Every message includes an opt-out line.

AI is used to draft and tune the wording of your replies. You approve the wording before anything goes live.
