Why speed matters

  
A study published in Harvard Business Review in 2011 found that companies who responded to an online enquiry within an hour were about 7 times more likely to have a meaningful conversation with the customer than those who waited even one hour longer. Most small firms take far longer than that, simply because they're busy doing the work.

  
Source: Oldroyd, McElheran & Elkington, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", HBR, March 2011.

FAQ
Questions people ask

 
Will it sound like a robot?
No. You approve the wording before it goes live, and it reads like a short note from you. You can try it on yourself above and judge.

 
Do I need a new website or app?
No. It works with the form, email inbox and calendar you already use.

 
What if a customer asks something tricky?
The reply sticks to a friendly acknowledgement, a few questions and your booking link. It doesn't quote prices or promise anything. You get the alert and take it from there.

 
What if it doesn't work out for me?
If you're not happy within 14 days of it going live, reply to any email and I refund the $350 setup fee in full. No forms, no argument.

 
Can I cancel?
Yes, any month, by replying to any email. No contract.

 
You're in India. What about time zones?
The replies run automatically around the clock, so time zones don't matter for your customers. For setup calls, I work evenings in US and Australian time.
